About Us

Building Quality. Creating Better Spaces.

At IYKESON Bathroom Affairs and Tiles Depot, we believe that every building deserves quality materials, thoughtful choices and products that combine durability with style.

Located in Akure, Ondo State, Nigeria, IYKESON is a building materials and home-improvement store committed to providing customers with quality products for residential and commercial projects. Our product range includes doors, floor and wall tiles, WC, sanitary wares, bathroom fittings, plumbing materials and other essential building products.

We cater to homeowners, contractors, builders, property developers and individuals looking to build, renovate or improve their spaces. Whether you are working on a new project or upgrading an existing property, we aim to make finding the right products easier by offering a variety of options to suit different needs, preferences and budgets.

Introducing Best Life Door

As part of our commitment to quality and innovation in the door industry, we proudly present Best Life Door, our dedicated door brand.

Best Life Door focuses on producing distinctive and quality door solutions, combining modern designs, functionality and craftsmanship. Our product range includes smart doors, fire-resistant doors, extra-height doors, extra-width doors and other specialised door solutions.

Beyond our own manufactured products, we also source and supply carefully selected doors, including Turkish, German, cast, copper and other distinctive designs, giving customers access to a wider range of choices.

Our Commitment

At IYKESON and Best Life Door, our goal goes beyond selling building materials. We are committed to helping customers make confident choices by providing quality products, reliable service and excellent value.

We understand that every project is different, and we strive to offer solutions that meet the expectations of our customers.
